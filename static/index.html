It's not about having lots of money. It's about knowing what to do with what you have.

We learn a lot at university. Money should be part of that.

Your allowance, your first job, a tempting loan, that “urgent” mobile money message — financial decisions don't wait until graduation.

The choices you make with money during university can shape your options long after graduation.

01 Why should I budget if my allowance is small?

Because even a small amount has to cover real needs. Planning helps you spot patterns, prepare for essential costs and decide what matters most to you.

02 Is saving only for people with jobs?

No. Saving is a habit, not a salary requirement. A little set aside consistently can support a small goal or help with an unexpected expense.

03 How do I know whether a money offer is safe?

Slow down, read the terms, verify the source and be suspicious of pressure or promises that sound too good to be true. Never share secret account codes.

04 Can I use the site without creating an account?

Yes. You can read the guides and use the planner without logging in. The site does not save your budget to a server. Any external saving-group form will clearly be identified.
